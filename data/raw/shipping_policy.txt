NovaMart Shipping Policy

NovaMart offers standard and express shipping options.

Standard shipping normally takes 5-7 business days from the date the order is shipped.

Express shipping normally takes 1-2 business days from the date the order is shipped.

Customers can view their shipping status and tracking information from their NovaMart account.

Orders placed before 2:00 PM on a business day are normally processed on the same business day.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM are normally processed on the next business day.

Shipping times may be longer during public holidays, major sales events, or unexpected delivery disruptions.

NovaMart currently provides shipping tracking for all orders after they are dispatched.

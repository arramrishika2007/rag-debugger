NovaMart Warranty Terms

NovaMart provides a 12-month limited warranty for eligible electronic products.

The warranty begins on the date the product is delivered to the customer.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures that occur during normal use.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, liquid exposure, unauthorized repairs, or intentional misuse.

Customers requesting warranty service must provide their order number and a description of the problem.

NovaMart may repair or replace an eligible product after inspecting the reported issue.

Replacement products are covered for the remaining period of the original warranty.

Warranty claims can be submitted through the NovaMart customer support portal.

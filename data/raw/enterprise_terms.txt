NovaMart Enterprise Terms

NovaMart provides additional services and terms for enterprise customers.

Enterprise customers have a 60-day refund window from the date an order is received.

Enterprise customers receive dedicated customer support through an assigned enterprise support channel.

Enterprise customers may have negotiated pricing based on their contract and order volume.

Enterprise accounts can request consolidated monthly invoices.

Enterprise customers can contact their dedicated support team for questions about orders, billing, refunds, and account management.

The specific terms of an enterprise customer's contract take precedence when they differ from the standard NovaMart policies.

Enterprise customers should provide their enterprise account ID when contacting dedicated support.

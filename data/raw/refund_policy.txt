NovaMart Refund Policy

NovaMart allows customers to request a refund within 30 days of receiving their order.

To be eligible for a refund, the product must be unused and returned in its original packaging.

Customers must submit a refund request through their NovaMart account. The request should include the order number and reason for the return.

Once NovaMart receives the returned product, the product is inspected before the refund is approved.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Products damaged due to customer misuse are not eligible for a refund.

Enterprise customers have a different refund window. Enterprise customers may request a refund within 60 days of receiving an order, subject to the enterprise agreement.

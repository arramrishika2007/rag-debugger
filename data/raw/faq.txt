NovaMart Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How can I check my order status?
A: Customers can check their order status and tracking information by signing in to their NovaMart account.

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping normally takes 5-7 business days after the order is shipped.

Q: How long does express shipping take?
A: Express shipping normally takes 1-2 business days after the order is shipped.

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Most products can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: After an approved return is received and processed, the refund is normally sent to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Q: How long is the warranty?
A: Eligible electronic products have a 12-month limited warranty starting from the delivery date.

Q: How can I contact NovaMart support?
A: Customers can contact support through the NovaMart customer support portal.
